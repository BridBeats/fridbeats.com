About Me

Frid Beats is a music producer, beatmaker, composer, artist and mixing engineer driven by creativity and sound.

I create original music and productions across genres such as Kompa, Afrobeat, Reggaeton, Dancehall, Trap, Drill, Boom Bap and more.

Whether I'm creating music for myself, building beats for artists, or developing tools for other producers, my goal is always the same: creating music with character, emotion and a strong creative direction.

Frid Beats is more than a beat store — it's a creative space where music, production and ideas come together.
