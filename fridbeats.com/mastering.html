QUESTIONS

Will mastering fix a bad mix?
Mastering is the final polish — it can't rebuild a mix. If your track has serious issues (clipping, mud, unbalanced vocals), I'll tell you honestly what to fix first. I'd rather keep you as a long-term client than sell you a master that sounds wrong.
What genres do you master?
Everything: trap, drill, afrobeat, kompa, reggaeton, dancehall, pop, R&B — instrumentals, full songs with vocals, loops, podcasts. The target (Sample Peak −0.01 dBFS, dynamics preserved) is universal because it follows the track, not a preset.
What do I send you after paying?
Your track (WAV preferred, MP3 accepted) and optionally a reference — a commercial song whose sound you want matched. Send via WhatsApp (+1 829 602 6502) or contact@fridbeats.com right after your order.
What exactly do I receive?
A mastered WAV 24-bit (for distribution: DistroKid, TuneCore, BeatStars...) + MP3 320k (for previews, socials) + a measurement report showing your track's before/after numbers.
Is my music safe?
100%. Your track stays private — it's only heard by me, used solely for your master, and never distributed or shared. The free analysis on this page runs entirely in your browser; the audio never leaves your device.
